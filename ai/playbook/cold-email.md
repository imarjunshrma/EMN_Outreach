# Cold-email writing playbook

## Research first

Research the prospect before writing.

Look at:

- Website.
- Social media.
- Content.
- Business model.
- Services.
- Marketing activities.
- Business positioning.

Identify ONE genuine, specific pain point.

Never invent observations.

## Writing style

- Simple English.
- Natural conversational wording.
- Three to five short lines.
- Avoid corporate language.
- Avoid generic AI expressions.
- Avoid unnecessary introductions.
- No excessive compliments.
- No complicated vocabulary.
- No exaggerated claims.
- Write as a real person would type.

## Business positioning

Elite Media Networks helps businesses grow through:

- Content strategy.
- Social media growth.
- Short-form video content.
- Performance marketing.
- Meta advertising.
- Google advertising.
- Lead generation.
- Business growth strategy.

We do NOT offer LinkedIn marketing.

Development and technology services should be positioned under the Bridgestone Technology brand, not Elite Media Networks.

Sell business outcomes:

- Leads.
- Qualified enquiries.
- Booked calls.
- Clients.
- Revenue opportunities.

Do not sell only views, impressions or video editing.

## Personalization

- Mention one real observation.
- Do not mention specific episode or post titles.
- Use natural phrases such as "I watched..." or "I noticed..."
- For business owners, ask about business results, not whether they personally create content.
- Do not force a connection between an unrelated observation and our services.

## Closing

End with a soft, relevant question.

No aggressive pitch.
No forced meeting request.
No generic Calendly link.
No hard selling.

The email should feel helpful and educational.

## Variants

Always generate three meaningfully different versions.

Each version should explore a different relevant conversational angle.

Do not simply change a few words.
