## Invite someone

:::steps
### Open the team page
Inside the workspace, open **Settings → Team → Invite**.

### Choose a role
Pick the lowest role that lets the person do their job. See [Core concepts](/docs/concepts) for what each role allows.

### Share the link
The invitation link is shown once and expires after seven days. The person opens it, signs in or creates an account, and joins this workspace only.
:::

> [!note]
> You must confirm your own email address before you can invite others. This keeps unverified accounts from sending invitations in your name.

## Organization roles

Organization owners and admins manage members across the organization in **Organization settings → Members**: change someone's organization role, or remove them from the organization and every workspace in one step. Only the owner can make someone an owner.

## Two-factor authentication

Everyone can turn on two-factor sign-in under **My account & 2FA**. Admins can require it for a workspace in **Settings → Security & AI policies**; members without it are asked to set it up before continuing.

## API keys

Workspace admins create API keys under **Settings → API keys**. A key belongs to one workspace, carries a role, can be limited to scopes such as `leads:read`, and can expire. It is shown once.
