The setup checklist appears after sign-up and stays available from **Organization settings → Setup checklist**. It never guesses: each tick comes from your actual configuration, so an item becomes done the moment the underlying setting exists.

| Step | Done when | Required |
|---|---|---|
| Organization details | The first workspace has a postal address and time zone | Yes |
| Business profile | Business knowledge or offer facts are written in AI Studio | Yes |
| Invite your team | At least one other member has joined | No |
| AI provider | A supported hosted or local text model is enabled | No |
| Connect a mailbox | At least one mailbox is connected | No |
| Sending domain & DNS | A domain is added and its records verified | No |
| Import leads | The workspace has at least one lead | No |
| First campaign | A campaign exists | No |

## Skipping steps

Optional steps can be skipped with **Skip for now**. Skipping only hides the reminder; it does not change any setting, and you can undo it. Required steps cannot be skipped, because emails without a postal address or without a business profile would be non-compliant or low quality.

> [!tip]
> You can leave the checklist with **Do this later** at any time. It returns the next time you sign in until the required steps are done.
