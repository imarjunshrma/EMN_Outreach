On the Agency plan an organization can hold many workspaces — one per client is the usual setup. Each client workspace has its own leads, campaigns, mailboxes, domains, AI knowledge, writing rules, branding, integrations and reports.

## Add a client

:::steps
### Create the workspace
Open **Organization settings → Workspaces → Add workspace** (open Organization settings from the organization name at the bottom of the sidebar, or from your profile menu) and name it after the client. The workspace counts toward your plan's workspace limit.

### Give it the client's identity
In the new workspace, open **Settings → Workspace** and enter the client's sender name and postal address. These appear in the client's email footers.

### Load the client's knowledge
In **AI Studio → Business knowledge**, describe the client's offer, audience and proof points. Knowledge never leaks between workspaces, so the AI cannot mix up two clients.

### Connect the client's mailboxes and domain
Connect mailboxes and verify the domain inside the client workspace. Credentials are encrypted and usable only there.

### Add the people who work on this client
Invite team members to this workspace only, with the role they need.
:::

## Who sees what

| Person | Sees |
|---|---|
| Organization owner or admin | Every client workspace |
| A member invited to one client workspace | Only that workspace — not the other clients, not billing |
| A client's own staff, if you invite them | Only their workspace, typically as Viewer |

> [!security]
> Inviting someone to a workspace never gives them access to your organization's other workspaces, billing or settings, and never gives you access to theirs.

## Billing

All client workspaces are billed together on your organization's subscription. Usage — emails, AI spend, verifications — is counted for the organization, and **Organization settings → Usage** breaks it down by month.
