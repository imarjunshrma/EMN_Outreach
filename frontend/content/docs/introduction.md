Outvexa is a cold-email platform built around one idea: every email should be researched, grounded in sources you can check, and approved by a person before it is sent. It covers the whole outbound workflow — finding prospects, researching them, writing, sending from your own mailboxes, and handling replies — for a single team or for an agency running many clients.

## How the product is organized

- **Organization** — your company. It owns the subscription, the billing details and every workspace. Each customer of the platform is a separate organization, and no data is shared between organizations.
- **Workspace** — a working area inside your organization: a sales team, a brand, or an agency client. Leads, campaigns, mailboxes, domains and AI settings belong to exactly one workspace.
- **Members** — people with a role in the organization, and a role in each workspace they work in.

> [!tip]
> One workspace is enough for most teams. Add more only when you need data kept apart — typically one per agency client.

## What the product will not do

Some outbound shortcuts damage deliverability and trust, so the product leaves them out by design: no mailbox rotation across throwaway accounts, no warm-up networks, no AI text sent without approval (unless you explicitly turn on an auto mode for a narrow case), and the AI never offers prices, discounts or deadlines that are not in your business knowledge.
