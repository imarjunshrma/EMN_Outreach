## The unified inbox

**Outreach → Inbox** collects replies from all mailboxes of the workspace. Each reply is classified — interested, wants pricing, not interested, has a provider, out of office, unsubscribe, complaint and others — so you can work the important ones first. Unsubscribes and complaints are suppressed immediately.

## Reply assistant modes

Set the mode under **Settings → Security & AI policies**.

| Mode | What happens |
|---|---|
| Suggest | A suggested answer appears next to the conversation. You write and send. |
| Draft (default) | Suggested answers go to an approval queue. Nothing is sent until someone approves. |
| Narrow auto-reply | For categories an admin enables, a *template you approved* is sent automatically, once per conversation. Free AI text is never sent automatically. |

> [!security]
> Pricing questions, objections, opt-outs, complaints, out-of-office notices and anything unclassified are never answered automatically, in any mode.

## Meetings

Add a booking integration (Calendly or Cal.com) under **Settings → Integrations** and point the tool's webhook at the address shown there; signatures are verified. Booked meetings are matched to the lead and campaign and appear in analytics and the pipeline.
