**Pipeline → Workflows** lets you build rules visually: *when* something happens, *if* conditions match, *then* do one or more actions.

## Triggers

A lead is added, verified, scored or changes status; a reply is categorised; an email bounces; a lead unsubscribes; a meeting is booked; a campaign pauses; a deal changes stage; every day; manually; or when an external system calls the workflow's webhook.

## Conditions

Compare lead country, status, score, title, tags or custom fields; company industry or size; the reply category; the campaign; or the weekday and hour. Conditions can branch into *then* and *else* paths.

## Actions

Add a tag, update the lead status, create a task, assign a user, create a deal, notify the team, research the lead, draft a reply, pause or resume a campaign, call a webhook, or export leads.

> [!note]
> Workflows cannot send email directly — sending always goes through campaigns and approval. Draft a reply instead, and it waits in the approval queue.

## Testing and limits

**Test run** shows what would happen without changing anything. Each workflow has a daily run limit, and each run counts toward your plan's monthly workflow allowance. A workflow never runs twice for the same event.
