title: Acceptable Use Policy
updated: 2026-11-21
---
> [!warning]
> **Draft for legal review.** Remove this notice once approved.

Outvexa is for honest, relevant business outreach. You may not use it to:

- Send email to people you have no lawful basis to contact, or keep emailing anyone who has opted out.
- Use purchased or scraped lists that the law in the recipient's country does not allow you to use.
- Hide who you are, use misleading subject lines, or send without a valid postal address and unsubscribe link.
- Send malware, phishing, illegal content, or content that harasses or discriminates.
- Rotate large numbers of mailboxes or domains to evade spam filters, or join warm-up networks through the Service.
- Probe, overload or attack the Service or other customers, or try to access data that is not yours.

We may pause campaigns or suspend organizations that break this policy, and will tell the organization owner why.
