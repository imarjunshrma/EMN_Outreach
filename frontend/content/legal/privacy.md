This policy explains how Elite Media Networks handles personal data in Outvexa.

## Two kinds of data

- **Account data** — about people who use the Service: name, email, password hash, sign-in records and billing contact. We are the controller of this data.
- **Customer Data** — leads, messages, research and other content our customers upload or create. The customer is the controller; we process it on their instructions to provide the Service.

## What we use account data for

To provide and secure the Service, send service emails (verification, password reset, billing), prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data.

## Processors

We use infrastructure, email-delivery and payment providers (Stripe, Razorpay) to run the Service. AI, mailbox, verification and data providers are connected by each customer with its own account and act on that customer's behalf.

## Retention

Account data is kept while the account exists and for a limited period afterwards for legal and security reasons. Customer Data is kept until the customer deletes it or its organization; deleted data leaves backups when they expire.

## Security

Credentials are encrypted, access between customers is isolated, and staff access to customer data is limited, time-boxed and recorded. See [Security](/security).

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your account data by writing to info@elitemedianetworks.com. If you are a lead contacted by one of our customers, contact that customer; we will forward requests we receive.
