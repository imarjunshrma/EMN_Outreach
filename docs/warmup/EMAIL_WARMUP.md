# Controlled email warm-up

Open **Mailbox health → Warm-up** and select a connected mailbox. Configure the sending days, timezone, window, pause dates, target, cap, interval, observations and a genuine reviewed message. Confirm enrollment, open the program, authorize the controlled recipients, then separately confirm **Start warm-up**. Mailbox connection and enrollment alone schedule no sends.

The suggested progression starts at the lower of the selected starting target, mailbox cap, and a conservative two-message ceiling. Custom mode uses the configured increment. Both require accepted submissions on distinct sending days, the minimum sample, and acceptable health. The sending engine applies the lowest applicable cap. These defaults are operating inputs and do not establish a universally safe volume.

Program, recipient and submission histories use separate records from prospect campaigns. See [operations](OPERATIONS.md) and [safety and limitations](SAFETY_AND_LIMITATIONS.md).
